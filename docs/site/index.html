OMEGA RATRACE 3D

Colby the marbled cat defends the Omega arena from mice, rats and the dreaded Death Rat. Now photoreal.

PLAY NOW3D-printable models

Features

Newtonian cat ship

Rotate, thrust and drift. Hairballs ricochet once off the rail walls. Same deterministic feel as the 2D original.

Waves of vermin

Mice, rats, cheese-pellet Commanders and mines. Linger past 42 seconds and the Death Rat comes for you.

Tuna yarn bomb

Grab tuna to earn a yarn bomb that clears the arena. Three lives, one high score.

Photoreal look

PBR materials, HDR image-based lighting, soft shadows, GTAO, bloom, SMAA and a film grade.

Blender-authored

Every model is built procedurally in Blender and validated as a watertight STL and a valid glTF.

Zero build, zero deps

Plain ES modules plus vendored three.js. Runs in any WebGL browser, no install.

Asset pipeline

Models are generated by Blender scripts (tools/blender/) and exported twice: a glTF binary (.glb, PBR materials, under a 40,000 triangle budget) for the game and a watertight STL for 3D printing. tools/validate-assets.mjs checks both (manifold edges, shells, minimum wall thickness, glTF structure) and writes assets/manifest.json.
